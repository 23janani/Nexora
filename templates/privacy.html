1. Scope and Commitment

This Privacy Policy explains how the Candidate Requirement Analysis Portal handles applicant resumes, contact information, and extracted evaluation data. We prioritize the safeguarding of candidate personally identifiable information (PII) and ensure strict compliance with applicable data privacy regulations, including GDPR and CCPA.

2. Information Processed by the System

When resumes are uploaded (via PDF, DOCX, DOC, TXT, or image formats), the system processes:

  • Contact Identifiers: Name, email address, phone number, and self-reported location for applicant tracking.
  • Professional History: Employment history, job titles, companies, technical duties, and quantified achievements.
  • Academic Qualifications: Degrees earned, fields of study, institutions, and graduation years.
  • Technical Competencies: Programming languages, software frameworks, tools, databases, and certifications.

3. Protected Characteristics Exclusion

The algorithmic pipeline strictly excludes sensitive categories. The system does not analyze, infer, or store data regarding racial origin, ethnic background, religious beliefs, political viewpoints, trade union membership, genetic data, biometric identifiers, health information, sexual orientation, or family status.

4. Local and Secure Processing

All natural language processing, vector similarity calculations, OCR extractions, and scoring routines execute on dedicated enterprise server infrastructure. Candidate data is never shared with, sold to, or harvested by third-party data brokers. Uploaded resume files are held in temporary memory or controlled local storage solely for the duration of the recruitment evaluation session.

5. Candidate Rights and Data Deletion

Candidates have the right to request review, correction, or permanent purging of their processed profile from internal systems. System administrators can trigger complete record erasure across the candidate repository upon written verified request.

6. Contact and Data Governance Officer

For inquiries regarding candidate data handling or privacy compliance standards, contact the Internal Talent Privacy Office at privacy-officer@recruitment.internal.
